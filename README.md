I built Kagoj (কাগজ, "paper") to change that: a free mobile guide that turns each process into a checklist, a sequence of steps and a direct link to the official portal.

How it works
→ Choose a service folder
→ Tick off the documents you have gathered
→ Follow the steps in order
→ Continue on the official website

Current coverage
NID correction, birth registration, e-passport, driving licence, land basics, TIN, trade licence, and a guide to finding offices in Chattogram.

Built to be trustworthy
• Content is drawn from government portals and published reports, and dated October 2026
• Fees are not listed, because they change; every page points to the official source
• The land section is general information, not legal advice
• Where I could not verify something, such as office addresses, I left it out
• No sign-up, and progress is stored only on the user's own device

What I learned: with public information, being clear about what you do not know matters as much as what you do.

I am looking for feedback from people who have been through these processes, and for legal and public-administration professionals willing to review the content.
